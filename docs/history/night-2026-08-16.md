## §1 — What the night found that nobody asked

1. **`api.agent_ptr` was a latent suite poison.** `test_cg_api.py` monkeypatched
   `lib.AgentStart` to return 77; `search_begin` cached that 77 as a MODULE
   GLOBAL that monkeypatch does not restore. The first test in the suite's
   history to call the real search API afterwards (S0.2, tonight) died as a
   segfault, not an assertion. Four tests now manage the global via
   monkeypatch — and two of them turned out to have silently DEPENDED on the
   leaked pointer.
2. **The LOCAL build's search arena segfaults.** Pointing `api.lib` at the
   local seeded engine so battle and search share one binary crashes in
   `SearchBegin`. Nothing in the plan needs it (the container runs the
   official binary), but S0.3's container probe is the only honest answer to
   the same-binary question.
3. **`record_corpus.py` wipes `records/` before writing** (`unlink` on every
   `registro_*.json`). Anyone repopulating the local corpus while the current
   records still matter loses them. Not run tonight; back up first by day.
4. **Half the crustle_wall losses are DECKOUTS, not prize races**: 42 losses
   in 200 games — 22 by prizes, **20 by deckout** — with 117 sterile turns
   concentrated in 41 of the 42. The wall does not out-race us; it out-lasts
   us. (`ogerpon_verde_1`: 14 losses in 200 — 11 prizes, 3 bench-out.)

## §3 — T2, the posterior (S1), shipped

`ptcg/opponent/prior.py`: hosting is a multiset-subset test and it is binary —
among lists that host the whole visible board, coverage is ALWAYS 100 %, so
the meta prior (`peso_meta` of `pesos.csv`) is what separates hosts, and the
board itself evicts wrong hosts as it grows. Zero is exact (the
`DeterminizationError` semantics); the coverage ranking only orders the
no-host fallback. `ids_seen` is an engine-free port of
`search_oracle._ids_seen`, held to 100 % parity by the census.

Census over the frozen corpus (50 records, 3 580 decisions, **8 archetypes** —
the "fifty Alakazam" memory is stale): all four criteria PASA; details in
`log/noche-2026-08-16/t2_censo.json`. Live records: 62/62 decisions hosted,
0 exceptions.

The plan's §5.3 criterion asked that the arbiter's choice grade positive on
the disagreement population and that the hard matchups not drag it down: the
balance is +64/−36 with the mean positive, and **crustle_wall leads the
ENDORSED side** (20 of 64, then festival_lead 19, alakazam 16) while also

both directions. The 226 floor abstentions are the design working: an
arbiter that answers everything is measuring its own noise. The 72 no-world
boards are the mid-effect class the determinizer's guard refuses.

The abstention -> S3: any live trigger must also count the 12 % of boards
where no world builds, and fire the fallback instantly there.
